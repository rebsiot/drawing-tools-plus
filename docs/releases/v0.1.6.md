# Drawing Tools+ v0.1.6 — All Drawings

## New
- **All Drawings (count):** A collapsed list immediately beneath **Locked Drawings**. Expand it to browse every Drawing on the current Scene.
- **Easy to identify:** Text, names, shape types, short IDs and map positions help distinguish similar markings.
- **More than 60 items:** Search all Drawings by text/name/type/ID; **Show More** reveals additional objects as needed.

## Actions
- **Locate** pans to and briefly highlights a Drawing.
- **Select** targets one unlocked Drawing; locked items offer **Unlock**.
- **Text** adds or changes a label on an unlocked Drawing.

## Good to know
- Style Presets and appearance controls (stroke, fill, line width and text) work as in v0.1.5.
- Opening or searching All Drawings does not change artwork, create new Drawings or unlock objects.
- **Beta:** Automated/mock checks passed, but live Foundry v14 confirmation of the inventory is still pending.
- Back up your world before updating. [v0.1.5](https://github.com/rebsiot/drawing-tools-plus/releases/tag/v0.1.5) remains available for rollback.
